01. Introduction and overview

Kliptra (the “App”, package com.kliptra.editor) is an Android video editor that helps you create, edit, enhance and export videos.

We designed Kliptra so that your photos, videos, audio and editing projects remain under your control. Core editing features—including supported captions, background removal, beauty adjustments, stabilization and export—run on your device.

Kliptra does not require an account and does not operate a first-party user analytics platform. However, integrated third-party SDKs may process device, advertising and diagnostic information as described below.

In short: Kliptra does not automatically upload your personal media or projects. Internet access may be used for advertising and optional resource downloads you request.

03. Advertising and Google AdMob

Kliptra integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). The current development version uses test ads; supported placements may include banners, full-screen ads and user-initiated rewarded ads when enabled.

Google may process information such as advertising identifiers (when available), IP address (which may reveal approximate location), device and application details, advertising interactions and diagnostics for advertising delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

Where required by applicable law, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Kliptra is intended to request relevant ad-consent choices through Google's consent technology before loading applicable ads. When available, you can review those choices under Settings → Legal → Privacy choices.

Android also provides options for managing or deleting your device's advertising ID, depending on your operating system version.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data from partner apps.

04. Optional internet downloads

Most editing features are designed to work offline. Internet access may be used for the following optional content you select, as well as for integrated advertising services.

Market items

Effects, transitions, filters, animations, frames and other resources may be downloaded from GitHub release pages.

Fonts

Fonts not already included in the app may be fetched from the Google Fonts repository when selected.

Speech recognition models

For supported offline captions, you may download a compatible whisper.cpp model (approximately 31–181 MB depending on the model) from Hugging Face. You may instead import a compatible model file yourself.

These download providers may receive standard connection information, such as your IP address, request timing and the requested file. The downloads do not include the photos, videos, audio or projects you are editing.

05. On-device AI and Google ML Kit

Some beauty, face-detection and background-removal features use Google ML Kit. The supported ML models are bundled with the app and image processing occurs on your device; Kliptra does not send your images to Google for those detections.

Google's ML Kit libraries may nevertheless collect limited technical diagnostics or usage metrics, such as device/app information, API usage and performance measurements, subject to Google's applicable terms.

Read the Google ML Kit terms.

06. Feedback and problem reports

If you choose Send feedback or Report a problem, Kliptra can open your email app with a draft containing details such as the app version, phone model, Android version and, for problem reports, recent crash information.

You can review or edit the email before sending it. We receive this information only if you choose to send it, and use it to respond to your inquiry or investigate the issue. You can request deletion of correspondence we retain by contacting us.

07. Data storage, retention and deletion

Kliptra saves projects, downloaded resources, speech models and supported temporary files in device storage. Such information may remain until you delete it, clear app data or uninstall the app, subject to Android storage and backup behavior.

Where available, Settings → Storage lets you review and clear stored content such as projects, speech models and caches. Exported videos saved outside app-private storage may remain after uninstalling and must be removed separately.

Information processed independently by third-party services is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices.

08. Data sharing and security

Kliptra does not automatically share your personal media or projects with ad networks or download providers. Third-party services may receive limited information related to their functions:

  • Google AdMob: ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention.
  • Google ML Kit: limited technical usage and diagnostics.
  • GitHub, Google Fonts and Hugging Face: ordinary network information relating to content you request.

If you export or share content through another app or cloud provider, its own privacy policy may apply. Kliptra uses available Android storage protections and reasonable safeguards, but no device or network service can guarantee absolute security.

09. Children's privacy

Kliptra is not directed at children under 13. We do not require users to create accounts or submit registration details. Third-party advertising or diagnostic services may nonetheless process technical information as described in this policy.

Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the address below.

10. Your privacy choices

  • Control camera, microphone and media permissions through Android settings.
  • Delete projects, downloaded speech models or cached files using available app or device controls.
  • Manage advertising consent through Settings → Legal → Privacy choices, where applicable and available.
  • Manage your advertising ID through Android settings.
  • Contact us concerning information you voluntarily send, including feedback.

Depending on applicable law, you may have additional data-protection rights. Information independently processed by third parties may need to be managed through those providers.
